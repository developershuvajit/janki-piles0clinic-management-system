### 2.2 Clinical Content (Hydrocele)
- **What is Hydrocele**: Accumulation of fluid around the testicle inside the tunica vaginalis sac, causing painless or dragging swelling in the scrotum.
- **Diagnosis**: Physical transillumination test and Scrotal Ultrasound (USG).

### 3.2 Clinical Content (Hernia)
- **What is Hernia**: Protrusion of internal abdominal organs/fat through a weakened spot or defect in the surrounding muscle wall (*Hernia Swelling*).
- **Types Treated**:
  1. **Inguinal Hernia**: Groin region swelling (most common in men).
  2. **Umbilical / Paraumbilical Hernia**: Swelling at or near the belly button.
  3. **Ventral / Incisional Hernia**: Swelling occurring at the site of a past surgical scar.
- **Laparoscopic Keyhole Technique (TEP / TAPP)**: 3 micro-keyholes, reduced muscle trauma, insertion of premium lightweight 3D contour mesh, rapid recovery in 48 hours.
