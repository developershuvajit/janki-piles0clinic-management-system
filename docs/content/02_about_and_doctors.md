### About Us Complete Content

#### Clinic History & Journey
Founded over 15 years ago with a single vision to transform anorectal care in North India, **Janki Piles Clinic** has grown from a specialized daycare unit into a premier multi-branch network of Advanced Laser Proctology and General Surgery Centers across Uttarakhand and Punjab.

For decades, patients suffering from piles (hemorrhoids), anal fissures, complex fistulas, and pilonidal sinus endured painful open surgeries, weeks of bed rest, and high recurrence rates. Realizing the severe physical discomfort and emotional embarrassment associated with these conditions, Janki Piles Clinic pioneered the adoption of **German 1470nm Diode Laser Technology** in the region.

Today, our clinic network across **Dehradun, Haridwar, Roorkee, Bhaniyawala, Srinagar Garhwal, Haldwani, and Mohali** has successfully treated over **15,000+ patients**, restoring their comfort, confidence, and quality of life with zero scalpel cuts.

---

#### Vision & Mission

##### Our Vision
To be the most trusted, patient-centric center of excellence in Laser Proctology and Minimally Invasive General Surgery across India, recognized for surgical precision, zero-pain outcomes, and ethical medical care.

##### Our Mission
- **Patient Comfort First**: To provide 100% painless, stitchless, daycare laser solutions that allow patients to resume daily life within 24-48 hours.
- **Break Social Stigma**: To foster an empathetic, non-judgmental environment where patients—especially women—can seek timely proctological help without fear or hesitation.
- **Clinical Excellence**: To continuously integrate state-of-the-art global surgical technologies, modular laminar OTs, and evidence-based clinical protocols.
- **Affordable Care**: To ensure world-class laser proctology remains accessible to all socio-economic sections through transparent pricing and cashless insurance assistance.

---

#### Core Values That Drive Us
1. **Clinical Integrity & Ethics**: We recommend surgery only when conservative or medical management is insufficient.
2. **Empathy & Respect**: We respect patient privacy, providing confidential consultation suites and dedicated female support staff.
3. **Pioneering Technology**: We constantly upgrade our surgical infrastructure with gold-standard German laser equipment and digital video anoscopy.
4. **Safety First**: Strict adherence to NABH-aligned hygiene standards, sterile modular operation theatres, and zero-infection protocols.
