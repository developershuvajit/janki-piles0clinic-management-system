## CATEGORY 2: GENERAL TREATMENT & DIAGNOSTICS (13 FAQs)

#### Q13. What is Digital Video Anoscopy?
**Ans**: It is a painless, 2-minute diagnostic procedure using a tiny illuminated digital camera to project high-resolution magnified images of internal hemorrhoids or fissure cuts onto a monitor.

#### Q14. Is digital anoscopy painful?
**Ans**: No, it is performed gently using lubricating local anesthetic gel to ensure complete comfort.

#### Q15. What is the difference between Grade 1, 2, 3, and 4 Piles?
**Ans**: Grade 1 stays inside; Grade 2 comes out during stool and returns spontaneously; Grade 3 requires manual pushing back; Grade 4 remains permanently prolapsed outside.

#### Q16. Can piles be treated permanently without open surgical wounds?
**Ans**: Yes. German 1470nm Laser Hemorrhoidoplasty (LHP) shrinks pile nodes internally without scalpel cuts.

#### Q17. How do doctors diagnose Anal Fistula?
**Ans**: Through physical examination, digital probe evaluation, and an MRI Fistulogram scan for complex or recurrent cases.

#### Q18. What is the difference between Piles, Fissure, and Fistula?
**Ans**: Piles are swollen blood vessels; Fissure is a sharp skin tear/cut; Fistula is an infected tunnel discharging pus.

#### Q19. Can constipation cause both piles and fissures?
**Ans**: Yes, passing hard stool and excessive straining put extreme pressure on anal veins (piles) and tear the anal mucosa (fissure).

#### Q20. Is biopsy required for piles tissue?
**Ans**: Routine piles do not require biopsy, but excised tissue during general surgery may be sent for histopathology verification if clinically indicated.

#### Q21. Can piles cause lower back pain or leg pain?
**Ans**: Severe piles themselves do not directly cause back pain, but prolonged abnormal seating postures due to anal discomfort can cause pelvic strain.

#### Q22. How is Pilonidal Sinus diagnosed?
**Ans**: Clinical examination of small pinpoint opening holes in the tailbone cleft, sometimes accompanied by purulent discharge.

#### Q23. What diagnostic tests are needed before laser surgery?
**Ans**: Basic routine blood work (CBC, Blood Sugar, BT/CT, Viral Markers) and ECG clearance.

#### Q24. Can hemorrhoids dry up automatically?
**Ans**: Mild Grade 1 piles may subside temporarily with fiber and hydration, but structural prolapsed tissue (Grades 3/4) requires medical laser ablation.

#### Q25. How do I know if my rectal bleeding is serious?
**Ans**: Any bleeding from the rectum must be evaluated by a proctologist to rule out colorectal polyps, IBD, or malignancy.

## CATEGORY 3: LASER SURGERY TECHNOLOGY & SAFETY (15 FAQs)

#### Q26. How does German 1470nm Diode Laser technology work?
**Ans**: It delivers precise laser energy through a specialized fiber optic probe to coagulate diseased blood vessels and shrink tissue from within while sparing healthy surrounding sphincter muscle.

#### Q27. Is laser piles surgery 100% painless?
**Ans**: Pain is reduced by 90-95% compared to open surgery. Patients experience mild tightness for 24 hours managed with standard analgesics.

#### Q28. Are there any stitches involved in laser surgery?
**Ans**: No, laser surgery is completely stitchless and cut-free.

#### Q29. Will laser surgery damage my anal sphincter muscle?
**Ans**: No. Unlike open surgery, laser energy is targeted precisely, preserving 100% of sphincter muscle fibers and preventing incontinence.

#### Q30. Is local or general anesthesia used during laser surgery?
**Ans**: Short-acting regional or local anesthesia is used, ensuring you feel zero pain during the procedure.

#### Q31. How long does the laser procedure take inside the OT?
**Ans**: Approximately 20 to 30 minutes.

#### Q32. What is the recurrence rate after laser piles surgery?
**Ans**: Under 1% when following recommended post-op diet and bowel habits.

#### Q33. Is laser surgery safe for heart patients or blood thinner users?
**Ans**: Yes, laser surgery causes minimal bleeding. Our team coordinates blood thinner adjustments with your cardiologist prior to surgery.

#### Q34. What is FiLaC laser procedure for fistula?
**Ans**: Fistula-tract Laser Closure (FiLaC) destroys infected fistula lining using 360° radial laser energy, causing the tunnel to collapse and heal.

#### Q35. What is SiLaC laser procedure for pilonidal sinus?
**Ans**: Sinus Laser Closure (SiLaC) cleans and seals tailbone sinus tracts without large open excisions or painful daily dressings.

#### Q36. Can laser surgery be performed if I have active bleeding piles?
**Ans**: Yes, laser energy actually seals bleeding vessels immediately during the procedure.

#### Q37. Why is laser surgery superior to conventional open surgery?
**Ans**: Zero open wounds, minimal pain, no stitches, lower infection risk, same-day discharge, and rapid 24-48h recovery.

#### Q38. Are laser equipment single-use and sterile?
**Ans**: Yes, optical fibers and patient contact accessories are single-use, medical-grade, sterile components.

#### Q39. Is laser surgery performed on a daycare basis?
**Ans**: Yes, patients walk into the clinic in the morning and return home comfortably the same afternoon.

#### Q40. Can laser surgery be done for recurrent fistula failed elsewhere?
**Ans**: Yes, Janki Piles Clinic specializes in complex recurrent cases using high-precision laser mapping.

## CATEGORY 6: DIET, NUTRITION & LIFESTYLE (13 FAQs)

#### Q68. What is the ideal daily dietary fiber intake to prevent piles?
**Ans**: Adults should consume 25 to 35 grams of dietary fiber daily through whole fruits, green vegetables, oats, and legumes.

#### Q69. Should I avoid spicy food completely if I have piles or fissure?
**Ans**: Yes, red chillies, raw spices, and hot sauces irritate inflamed anal mucosal lining, worsening pain and burning sensations.

#### Q70. How much water should I drink daily to keep stools soft?
**Ans**: Drink at least 3 to 4 liters of clean water daily, increasing intake during summer months.

#### Q71. Is tea or coffee harmful for piles patients?
**Ans**: Excessive caffeine causes bowel dehydration and gastrointestinal hypermotility. Limit tea/coffee to 1-2 small cups daily.

#### Q72. Can drinking warm water in the morning relieve morning constipation?
**Ans**: Yes, drinking 2 glasses of warm water on an empty stomach stimulates the gastrocolic reflex, promoting natural urge.

#### Q73. Are prunes, papaya, and apples good for piles?
**Ans**: Yes, papaya contains papain enzymes and prunes contain sorbitol, both of which act as natural, gentle stool softeners.

#### Q74. Can I eat rice and wheat roti if I suffer from piles?
**Ans**: Yes, but choose whole wheat roti (atta with bran) or brown rice over refined white flour (maida).

#### Q75. Is alcohol consumption bad for piles and fissure?
**Ans**: Yes, alcohol dehydrates the body, strains liver vascular function, and leads to hard, painful stools.

#### Q76. How long should I sit on the toilet commode?
**Ans**: Never sit on the toilet commode for longer than 3 to 5 minutes. Prolonged sitting increases pelvic vein engorgement.

#### Q77. Is using a smartphone on the toilet bad for piles?
**Ans**: Yes! Using phones in the bathroom distracts you and increases sitting time, directly triggering hemorrhoidal prolapse.

#### Q78. Can yoga exercises help prevent piles recurrence?
**Ans**: Yes, pelvic floor strengthening exercises (Ashwini Mudra, Kegels, Pawanmuktasana) improve local circulation and sphincter muscle tone.

#### Q79. Is curd (dahi) or buttermilk (chaas) recommended for piles?
**Ans**: Yes, probiotics in buttermilk and curd improve gut microbiome health and ease digestion.

#### Q80. How does a toilet footstool help during bowel movements?
**Ans**: Elevating feet on a small stool creates a natural 35° squatting angle, relaxing the puborectalis muscle for effortless evacuation.

## CATEGORY 7: SPECIAL CONDITIONS: WOMEN, ELDERLY & CHRONIC CASES (10 FAQs)

#### Q81. Why are women more prone to piles and fissures during pregnancy?
**Ans**: Growing fetal weight increases pelvic vein pressure, while progesterone hormones slow gut motility, triggering severe constipation.

#### Q82. Is laser piles surgery safe during breast-feeding / lactation?
**Ans**: Yes, local/regional anesthesia and safe medications ensure zero interruption to lactation or infant safety.

#### Q83. Can elderly patients above 70 years undergo laser surgery safely?
**Ans**: Yes, laser surgery is daycare, non-invasive, and minimally traumatic, making it the safest option for senior citizens.

#### Q84. What if a patient has both Piles and Fissure simultaneously?
**Ans**: Our senior proctologists can perform combined Laser Hemorrhoidoplasty and Laser Sphincterotomy in a single 30-minute session.

#### Q85. Can uncontrolled diabetes complicate piles recovery?
**Ans**: Uncontrolled blood sugar delays wound healing. Because laser surgery creates no open wounds, infection risk is minimized, but sugar levels are optimized pre-op.

#### Q86. How is piles managed in patients taking blood thinning medications like Aspirin?
**Ans**: Blood thinners are temporarily paused or bridged under cardiologist guidance prior to the procedure.

#### Q87. Can severe piles cause erectile dysfunction or urinary issues in men?
**Ans**: Piles do not directly cause ED, but severe perianal pain and fear of bowel movements can cause pelvic muscle spasm and reflex urinary hesitancy.

#### Q88. Why do young college students get anal fissures?
**Ans**: Irregular meal timings, junk food diets (maida/burgers), low water intake, late-night sleeping, and acute hard constipation.

#### Q89. Can piles develop inside the body without any visible external lump?
**Ans**: Yes, Grade 1 and early Grade 2 internal piles lie hidden inside the anal canal and manifest only as painless bright red bleeding.

#### Q90. What if I have had multiple failed fistula surgeries elsewhere?
**Ans**: Complex recurrent fistulas are evaluated with 3D MRI Fistulogram. FiLaC laser closure successfully treats recurrent tracts without sphincter damage.

## CATEGORY 8: EMERGENCY & RECTAL BLEEDING RED FLAGS (10 FAQs)

#### Q91. What should I do if blood is spurting continuously from the anus?
**Ans**: Lie down flat, elevate your hips on a pillow, apply clean gauze pressure to the perianal area, and call our emergency helpline (`+91 98765 43210`) immediately.

#### Q92. What is a Thrombosed External Hemorrhoid?
**Ans**: A painful purple blood clot forming inside an external pile mass. It causes sudden, severe excruciating pain and requires immediate laser evacuation.

#### Q93. What is a Perianal Abscess and why is it a medical emergency?
**Ans**: A painful, swollen, pus-filled collection near the anus accompanied by fever and throbbing pain. Immediate surgical drainage is vital to prevent severe tissue destruction.

#### Q94. Can black or maroon blood in stool be piles?
**Ans**: No. Bright red blood comes from lower anorectal issues (piles/fissure). Black or dark maroon stool (*Melena*) indicates upper gastrointestinal bleeding and requires emergency endoscopy.

#### Q95. What if prolapsed piles cannot be pushed back inside manually?
**Ans**: This is a Grade 4 strangulated pile emergency. Delay can lead to tissue death (gangrene). Contact our clinic immediately.

#### Q96. Can severe rectal bleeding cause fainting or shock?
**Ans**: Yes, acute heavy blood loss leads to sudden blood pressure drop, dizziness, and collapse. Emergency fluid resuscitation and hemostatic care are required.

#### Q97. Is fever with anal pain dangerous?
**Ans**: Yes. High fever accompanied by throbbing perianal pain strongly indicates a deep pelvic or perianal abscess requiring urgent drainage.

#### Q98. How quickly can Janki Piles Clinic attend to an emergency patient?
**Ans**: Our emergency surgical team and OT are available on call 24/7 for immediate admission and intervention.

#### Q99. What immediate home first-aid can be done for severe anal fissure pain?
**Ans**: Sit in a hot water sitz bath for 15 minutes, apply prescribed local anesthetic gel gently, and refrain from straining.

#### Q100. Who can I contact right now for an urgent proctology medical emergency?
**Ans**: Call our Direct Emergency Surgical Line: `+91 98765 43210`.
